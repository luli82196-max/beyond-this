# Seed v1.2 audio placeholders

Place licensed audio here, then set paths in `src/systems/audio/seedAudio.ts`.

- `ambient`: low, nearly still earth ambience; loopable.
- `soil`: one restrained, dry-soft seed impact.
- `water`: one close, small droplet contact; no splash or magical tail.
- `wet_soil`: restrained absorption and damp granular cohesion after contact.
- `subtle_environment_change`: almost subliminal tonal shift as the changed soil reaches the seed; no magical rise.
